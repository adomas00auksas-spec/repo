Walk the whole of Japan, from castle towns to mountain passes. Learn a trade, serve the shogun or defy him. Every new life brings its own era, its own shogun and its own war.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or tap the ground. Press E or Space to enter buildings and speak with people. M opens the map, Q your quests.
