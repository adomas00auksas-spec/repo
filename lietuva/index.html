Lietuvos gyvenimas

Grow up anywhere from Vilnius to the dunes of Nida. Go to school, chase bottle money, join a drift club, or climb the street ranks from pacanas to gangsteris. Eight towns, one open country.

WASD or arrows to move. E talk and enter, F get in a car, J or click to punch, Space handbrake. M map, Q quests, T phone.
Tik žaidimas · Just a game

This is fictional role-play. Gangs, cartels, street racing, fights and contraband in this game are made up. Do not copy any of it in real life. Real gangs and illegal racing ruin and end lives.

Tai išgalvotas vaidmenų žaidimas. Nekartokite to realiame gyvenime. Gaujos, kontrabanda ir gatvės lenktynės realybėje sugriauna gyvenimus.

All gangs, crews, clubs and characters are invented. Real places appear only as scenery.
