Our story

A small room with a big fire.

Ember & Oak began with a single brick oven and a promise: cook only what the season offers. Ten years on, we still buy whole animals, bake bread every morning and source vegetables from farms within 60 miles.

Pull up a chair at the chef's counter and watch dinner happen.

— Chef Nora Bellweather
