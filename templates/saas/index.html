Questions, answered

How long does setup take?

Most teams send their first events in under 15 minutes using our snippet or a warehouse connection.

Do I need to know SQL?

No. Ask in plain English. If you love SQL, you can still write it and save queries for the team.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month unless you choose yearly billing, and you can export all your data whenever you want.

Where is my data stored?

In the US by default, or in the EU on request. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
